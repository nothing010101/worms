How it works
The dapp reads $WORMS holders. Eligible wallets (≥0.5% of supply; on a 100B token / 18 decimals reference that is 500M tokens at the line) become worms whose on-screen size grows with holding weight. Drop below 0.5% and you vanish next refresh. Rewards are automatic and distributed by the dev — holders do nothing.
